What is an OPG scan?
An OPG (Orthopantomogram) is a special dental X-ray that shows a full view of your mouth in a single image.
It captures:
•	All your teeth (upper and lower)
•	Jawbones
•	Temporomandibular joints (TMJ)
•	Surrounding structures
Unlike small dental X-rays that show only a few teeth, an OPG gives a complete panoramic view. This helps dentists spot problems that may not be visible otherwise.
